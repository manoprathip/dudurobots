[ WHAT IS DUDU ]

Delivery that moves
with your business.

DUDU Robots brings autonomous delivery into the places where people shop, work and eat. DUDU is the delivery technology and operating concept; Go Spesa is one real-world use case for the platform. Our goal is simple: help organisations move orders more efficiently while creating a modern customer experience.

From restaurants and retailers to shopping-centre operations, DUDU can connect the point of order with the point of delivery — while creating an additional opportunity for brand visibility.

HOW IT WORKS

From order
to destination.

A simple operational flow designed to fit into the everyday rhythm of a shopping centre or indoor delivery environment.

01

ORDER

A customer or employee places an order.

02

LOAD

The restaurant or store places the order inside DUDU.

03

DELIVER

DUDU navigates autonomously through the environment.

04

ARRIVE

The recipient receives the order at the destination.

05

RETURN

DUDU returns ready for the next delivery.

PILOT FOCUS / BUSNAGO, ITALY

Starting in Busnago.
Building for Italy.

DUDU's initial use case is focused on autonomous food and retail delivery inside Centro Commerciale Globo, Busnago. The concept connects restaurants, shops, employees and customers through a practical last-metre delivery service.

The pilot is designed to learn from real operations and create a model that can be adapted to other shopping-centre environments.
